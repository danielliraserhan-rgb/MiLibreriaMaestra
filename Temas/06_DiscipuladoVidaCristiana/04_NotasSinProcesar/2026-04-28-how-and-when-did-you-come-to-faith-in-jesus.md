# How and when did you come to faith in Jesus

> Versión en español: [[2026-04-28-como-y-cuando-llego-a-la-fe-en-jesus|¿Cómo y cuándo llegó a la fe en Jesús?]]

## Contenido

I was born into a Muslim family in [[Mexico]]. My grandfather built the first mosque there. So, as I grew up, the name [[Jesucristo|Jesus]] just represented those who mistakenly thought a prophet was God. I lived as a Muslim for 18 years until I left for college in the United States. Being in a different culture, I saw how everyone felt so "free and happy," while I had to hold back and perform many rituals in a language that wasn't really mine and that I didn't fully understand. Plus, I was studying engineering and deeply believed in science and logic. I started doubting my Muslim faith but not enough to stop being a Muslim.

Over time, I became more liberal, interpreting my faith not just through the Quran or Islamic teachings but through my own opinions. Everything else in life seemed to be going perfectly. I moved to a new house with new friends, college life was great, and I still maintained a strong relationship with my family. Overall, my life was perfect.

One day, I decided to visit my then-girlfriend in Mexico City because she was studying there. I left the States and went to Mexico. When I was returning to Dallas, I was stopped at the border and kept for about seven hours, handcuffed to a chair, waiting for an explanation of why I couldn't get back home. Unfortunately, I never received an answer and had to go back to my parents' house with no visa, no home, and no friends. That's where everything changed.

I began questioning life so deeply that I eventually denied its meaning altogether. Life seemed purposeless; we were merely here by accident, and after we die, we just return to dust, continuing the cycle. There was no God, no purpose, no meaning, no direction—just life.

Later, I moved to [[Monterrey]] and enrolled in a "Transformational Leadership Program." This changed my life, as I started adopting new age philosophy, believing that life itself had no inherent meaning and that I could create my own. I even thought I could, with my words, shape my reality and others' as well. So influenced by this philosophy, I became a life coach, founded my own leadership school, and studied its teachings more and more. Again, my life seemed perfect. Not only was it perfect, but I was also teaching others how to make their lives perfect.

But inside, I felt empty. Each night, the emptiness felt as deep as ever. This philosophy started to make everything so abstract that it lost its meaning again. I felt like I was climbing a mountain only to reach a precipice, and if I took one more step, I'd fall into the abyss. My life began to spiral downward, yet I continued to teach leadership all over Mexico. Life felt utterly horrible—no meaning, no purpose, no life—just moving forward by inertia.

Suddenly, my dad became a Christian and started talking to me about Jesus. Remember, Jesus was just a mistake for me, so I never took what my dad said seriously. But he gave me a book called "The Stranger on the Road to Emmaus." I started reading it skeptically, but it soon became my refuge. The message in this book was incredibly beautiful. How can it make so much sense if it contradicts all my beliefs?

I began craving this truth, this message, and even Jesus himself.

But wait, I couldn't bear to be disappointed one more time. This longing for truth turned into hunger and thirst, so I researched the historical background of this guy called Jesus. Apparently, he did live on Earth and started an incredible journey. Next was the question of the validity of his book, the Bible. Well, historically, it is one of the most reliable books on Earth. Plus, there was extra-biblical evidence supporting it. My life was upside down now. Everything I once believed was being questioned. Was it possible that Jesus really is God? Wait, is it possible that God exists? Does he want me to know Him? If Jesus is indeed a historical figure, why did no one teach me about Him? I went to a Christian retreat where they made a call: "If there is anyone who is too tired of life and wants to rest in Jesus, anyone who wants to accept Jesus into their life, please come forward." So, I did. After this, a woman said she felt there was someone in the room thinking of taking his life, and if he wanted her to pray for him, he just had to raise his hand. So I did. This was the most incredible day of my life, until it wasn't. The next day, I thought to myself that it was just a game and that all those feelings were just made up in my mind. Questions and more questions invaded my life and my mind. By then, I was spending more time researching, studying, watching sermons, and reading Christian books than I was doing my college homework. But I still did not want to surrender. I thought, if this is possible, then all religions are possible, and I'm not going to fall again emotionally for the first thing I encounter. My journey to "find the truth" had begun.

I started interviewing anyone who seemed available. I questioned my Muslim family, some Hindu friends, new age teachers, my own transformational leadership teachers, Buddhists, Mexican shamans, atheists, etc. I spoke to everyone who seemed to have an idea of a belief system. All these belief systems apparently had a coherent idea at first, and they all made sense. But as I questioned them more deeply, the arguments began to crumble and fall. They couldn't stand on their own.

I then went to a second Christian retreat, one year after the first. My purpose in this one was to put God on trial. When they made the call, I was going to step forward and pray with all my heart to be filled with the Holy Spirit. So, I did. Nothing happened. No feelings like last time, no words, no nothing. I felt so disappointed. I couldn't bear this anymore. I went to speak to who later became my youth pastor. At this time, he had just opened a channel of communication where I could ask him any question I had. So, I did.

To my surprise, he could give answers to the most profound questions I could think of. No matter what I asked, he responded through [[Jesucristo|Jesus Christ]]. It was unbelievable. So, I began longing for Jesus in the deepest parts of my heart. I had been longing for him for two years, but he never answered. This time, I couldn't even sleep. Every night, I prayed for him to answer, to speak to me, to call me his son. But this never happened. I spent four years studying about him and other religions, four years praying every night to hear his voice or see his face. I even asked for dreams and visions, for an angel, for anything he could give me to show it was him, but it never happened.

How could I become a Christian if the entire religion is based on a relationship with God, and this God does not speak to me? He seems to talk to others—everyone else but not me. By this point, I knew everything about Jesus, all the evidence supporting his existence and miracles. I knew that his book was the only reliable one and that his message was the only one that made sense. I even wanted to believe in and accept him so badly, but he had chosen to stay silent. How can I dedicate my life to someone who does not speak to me?

Finally, I moved in with a new roommate—a friend from my childhood who was also searching for truth. After reading the Bhagavad-gītā (Hindu scripture), we decided to read the Bible. My father had given me a study Bible with commentary by McArthur. As we began reading it, it blew our minds instantly. We started with the Gospels and spent two to four hours discussing them every day. Still, this amazing God did not speak to me.

One day I grabbed my bible and went to my room. I started reading [[Juan|John]] 4. Here we had Jesus speaking with a Samaritan woman, guilty of adultery, about how he was the water that would quench all thirst. Suddenly, as if the words came to life I could hear God's voice:

> "Jesús le dijo: Si supieras que yo soy quien habla contigo."
> [[Juan 4:26]] RV60.

> "Yeshua tells her, 'I - the one speaking to you - I am'"
> John 4:26 TV.

In that moment, I became a follower of [[Jesucristo|Jesus Christ]]. It was as if I traveled through time and saw Him speaking to me about seven years earlier. He was the one who took me outside my house, removed the Islamic ideas from my mind, and made me realize that the world's philosophy is empty. He led me on a journey to find the truth. Throughout this time, He was the one speaking to me. From that day on, I surrendered everything I thought I was and started from nothing, knowing only that He is God, my Lord, and my Savior.

Soon after, I met [[Miguel Pla]], who became my pastor, he is now part of Kingdom Living and Tikkun. He taught me about the love of the Jewish people and the Hebrew/Jewish narrative in the Bible. I later studied at Emmaus Online, a Frontier Alliance International training program, and graduated during a visit to Israel.

I am now the director of our Bible school and serve as the youth pastor in our church. I feel the need to study historical studies more deeply to understand how a first-century Jew would have perceived the gospel at the time of Jesus' coming.
